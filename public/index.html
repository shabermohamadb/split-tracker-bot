Discord Bot Setup & Help

The Split Tracker Discord bot helps guild members query their split balances from this spreadsheet directly from Discord.

1

Link Your Account

Run the /linkign <your_ign> command in Discord. This maps your Discord ID to your Albion Online character name.

2

Check Your Balance

Use the /mysplit command in Discord to instantly view your details or click the Refresh button to update them.

3

Receive Notifications

Every time a split leader updates the spreadsheet, the bot automatically sends you a direct message (DM) showing your balance updates, split values, and formulas.

Useful Commands Reference

/linkign [ign] - Maps your account (Case-Insensitive).

/mysplit - Shows your active silver balance.

/unlinkign - Unlinks your Discord account.

/sync (Admin Only) - Force-polls spreadsheet updates.

/viewsplits (Admin Only) - View sorted balance directories.
